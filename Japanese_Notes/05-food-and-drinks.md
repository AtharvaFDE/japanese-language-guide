## 🔗 2. The Connector と (To)

**と (To) = And**

Used to connect two nouns.

```text
A + と + B = A and B
```

Example:

```text
すし と みず
Sushi to mizu
Sushi and water
```

<details>
<summary>💡 Learn more about と</summary>

- Connects two nouns.
- Means "and" in English.
- Used with food, drinks, and people.

</details>
