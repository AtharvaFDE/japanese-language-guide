# 🇯🇵 Japanese Vocabulary & Sentences – Food & Drinks 

## 🍱 1. Vocabulary

| Japanese | Kanji | Romaji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🍚 ごはん | ご飯 | Gohan | Rice |
| 🍣 すし | 寿司 | Sushi | Sushi |
| 💧 みず | 水 | Mizu | Water |
| 🍵 おちゃ | お茶 | Ocha | Green tea |

## 📚 2. Grammar

### と (To) = And

Connects two nouns, food, drinks, or people.

**Formula:**
```text
A + と + B = A and B
```

**Example:**
```text
みず と すし
Mizu to sushi
Water and sushi
```
