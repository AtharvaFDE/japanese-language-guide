# 🇯🇵 Japanese Vocabulary – Food & Drinks

## 🍱 1. Vocabulary

| Japanese | Kanji | Romaji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 💧 みず | 水 | Mizu | Water |
| 🍣 すし | 寿司 | Sushi | Sushi |
| 🍵 おちゃ | お茶 | Ocha | Tea |
| 🙏 ください | - | Kudasai | Please |
| 🍚 ごはん | ご飯 | Gohan | Rice |

## 📚 2. Grammar

### と (To) = And

Connects two nouns.

```text
みず と すし
Mizu to sushi
Water and Sushi
```

### です (Desu) = Is / It is

Makes a polite statement.

```text
みず です。
Mizu desu.
It's water.
```
